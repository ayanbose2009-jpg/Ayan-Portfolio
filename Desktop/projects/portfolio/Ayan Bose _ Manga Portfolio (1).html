CHAPTER 1

About Me

I am Ayan Bose, a first-year B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (Core) student at VIT Vellore and an alumnus of Amity International School, Mayur Vihar, where I completed my Class X and XII. I have a keen interest in technology, artificial intelligence, and prediction models, and I enjoy exploring how computational tools can solve real-world problems. During the COVID-19 pandemic, I developed an AI-based health bot, which sparked my interest in applying technology to practical challenges. I have also participated in the Wharton Business School Youth Programme, gaining exposure to business concepts and collaborative problem-solving. Beyond academics, I served as my school’s Head Boy and am a core member of the Vincent MUN Society. Having won five Model United Nations (MUN) conferences, I enjoy public speaking, debating, and engaging with diverse perspectives. I believe that listening to different opinions is essential for developing a well-rounded understanding of complex issues.

Outside academics, I hold a black belt in karate, play district-level football, and have won interschool chess competitions twice. These experiences have helped me develop discipline, strategic thinking, teamwork, and perseverance. I also qualified for Round 2 of the Vasudha Science Fest, reflecting my enthusiasm for scientific exploration and problem-solving. In addition, I have participated in social initiatives, including donation drives, through which I have contributed to community welfare. I enjoy working with people, learning from new experiences, and challenging myself in unfamiliar environments. I am eager to expand my technical knowledge, collaborate with like-minded individuals, and explore opportunities that allow me to combine technology, leadership, and creativity to make a meaningful impact.
